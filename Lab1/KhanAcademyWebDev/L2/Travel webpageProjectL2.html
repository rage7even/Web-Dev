Travel to Kazakhstan

Discover Kazakhstan for yourself

Kazakhstan is a country of endless steppes, majestic mountains, and ancient cultural traditions. Here, East meets West, and modernity blends harmoniously with history. A trip to Kazakhstan will give you unforgettable impressions!

What to see?

  • Astana — a futuristic capital with the Baiterek Tower
  • Almaty — the cultural heart of the country at the foot of the mountains

Kazakh cuisine

  1. Beshbarmak — a traditional dish made with meat and noodles
  2. Kumys — a refreshing drink made from mare's milk
  3. Baurstaki — light and fluffy fried dough pieces
