Recipe #1: Beshbarmak

  • Time: 2 hour
  • Servers: 6
Ingredients: Quantity:
Lamb & beef 1.5-2kg
Onions 4-5 large
Salt 2 tsp
Water(for dough) 150 mg

Step 1: Place lamb or beef in a large pot, cover with water

Step 2:Add salt, bay leaves, and simmer for 1.5–2 hours until tender.

Step 3: Skim off foam during cooking.

Step 4: Boil the squares in the meat broth for 5–7 minutes until soft.Remove and drain.

Step 5: Slice onions into rings.Simmer in a ladle of broth with black pepper until translucent.

Step 6: Lay noodles on a large platter.Place meat (cut into chunks) on top.Pour onion sauce over everything.Serve hot, traditionally eaten with hands (“beshbarmak” means five fingers).

Recipe #2: Kurdak

  • Time: 1.5 hour
  • Servers: 4-6
Ingredients: Quantity
Lamb & beef Potatoes
Oninons 500 g
Black pepper 1 tsp
Garlic 3-4 cloves
Salt 1.5 tsp
Water 100 mg

Step 1:Cut lamb or beef into medium cubes (2–3 cm).Heat oil or rendered fat in a heavy pan/kazan.Fry meat until browned on all sides.

Step 2:Slice onions into rings, add to meat, and sauté until golden.

Step 3:Cut potatoes into large chunks.Add to the pan, mix with meat and onions

Step 4:Add a little water (½ cup) to prevent sticking.Cover and simmer on low heat for 40–50 minutes until potatoes are tender.

Step 5:Add crushed garlic and fresh herbs before serving.Traditionally served hot in a communal dish.
